Screencue Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

In short: Screencue records your screen, camera and microphone on your own computer and uploads the video directly to your Google Drive. We do not run servers that receive your recordings, we do not collect analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

This policy explains how the Screencue Chrome extension ("Screencue", "we", "us"), published by Hamza Khan, handles information when you use it.

Google Drive access

Screencue requests the drive.file permission. That means it can only see and change files and folders that Screencue itself created in your Drive. It cannot read, list or modify any of your other Drive files. Screencue uses this access only to create folders you ask for, upload your recordings, rename them, and optionally turn on link sharing when you enable the "Anyone with the link can view" setting.

Limited Use disclosure

Screencue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the recording and upload features you see in the extension. It is not transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, used to train machine-learning models, or read by humans.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers that receive your recordings, camera, microphone or screen data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • Screencue does not read the content of the web pages you visit. It only draws its camera bubble and recording controls on the page while a recording is in progress.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings live in your Google Drive and are under your control; delete them in Drive at any time. To remove everything Screencue stores locally, uninstall the extension. To revoke Drive access, use "Disconnect Google account" in Screencue's settings, or visit your Google Account permissions.

Children

Screencue is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
